1. Background & Context
   The brand name McRAYGOR has been active in the market since 2007 and has strong recall among
   municipal bodies, contractors, and export customers.
   Earlier, the brand operated under McRAYGOR Mechanicals Pvt. Ltd., which was later shut down due to
   business losses and legal issues. As a result, some outdated and negative information exists online related
   to the old private limited company.
   Since 2019, the business has been operating as McRAYGOR Mechanicals Infrastructure, a new and
   independent sole proprietorship entity with no legal or operational linkage to the previous company, apart
   from the brand name.
   The brand name has been retained deliberately due to: - Strong legacy recall - Existing customer trust -
   Long-standing presence in municipal and export markets

   Build two high-quality, modern websites (Indian & Global)

   Achieve top search rankings in India for sewer cleaning machines and related keywords
   Position McRAYGOR as a premium, technology-driven manufacturer
   2.2 Highest Priority
   Online Reputation Management (ORM) is the top priority of this project.
